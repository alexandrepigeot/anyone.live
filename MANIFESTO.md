# anyone.live ?

Do you guys remember when we used to say "the world is a village" ? The web was supposed to connect.

Looking around today, I don't see a village. I see a derelict mall. Our precious World Wide Web has become nothing more than a funnel for the machines.

But I want to go back to building that village. And I want to start with a system that allows anyone to "walk its streets".

So that's the basic idea: you open up the app, point to an address on the web and simply ask... anyone.live ? And maybe the world is a village again.

# FAQ (about the concept)

I have been the owner of anyone.live for more than 15 years. I've dreamt that project in every dimension possible.

So I've talked about it a lot and I've had lots of opinions handed to me. Sometimes, even useful ones.

## This is not safe !

Walking on the streets by yourself is not safe either.

You shouldn't accept candies from strangers. You shouldn't leave your kids unsupervised.

I intend to implement safety measures to give maximum privacy for the users and maximum transparency on the tools that they are using.

But I need everyone to understand also that this is a part of the humanity experiment. I don't control you. No one should.

## How do you turn a profit ?

I don't care about making a profit. I've never cared about looking successful or making money to be honest. I just need enough to feed my family and give a good life to my kids and I got a day job for that.

If paying for the services necessary for this platform to exist gets out of hand, I will start getting the community to contribute. But it will be a transparent process. By the people, for the people.

Step one will be to host it on a netbook. An Acer Aspire One from 2010, lol.
